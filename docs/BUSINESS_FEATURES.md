# Souklab — Platform Capabilities & Business Features

> A plain-language guide to what Souklab does, who it serves, and what every feature offers — written for product, business, and operations teams.

---

## What Is Souklab?

Souklab is an **online marketplace for Algerian artisans and their customers**. It gives skilled craftspeople a professional digital presence and a suite of tools to grow their craft business — while giving clients a single, trusted place to discover authentic Algerian craftsmanship, attend masterclasses, and connect with artisans directly.

---

## Who Uses Souklab?

| User Type | Who They Are | What They Come For |
|---|---|---|
| **Artisan** | A craftsperson — ceramicist, weaver, embroiderer, jeweller, woodworker, and more | A profile, audience, income from masterclasses, and direct client relationships |
| **Client** | A consumer interested in Algerian craft | Discovery, learning (masterclasses), messaging artisans, purchasing experiences |
| **Administrator** | Souklab platform staff | Ensuring quality, safety, and smooth platform operations |

## Core Platform Features

### 1. Artisan Profiles

Every artisan on Souklab gets a rich, professional profile that acts as their digital storefront.

**What artisans can do:**
- Write a detailed biography and describe their craft specialty
- Upload a portfolio gallery of up to **20 high-quality images** showcasing their work
- Add professional certifications (diplomas, awards, workshop credentials)
- Display their location (wilaya/city) so clients can find nearby artisans
- Set their contact details (phone and email) — visible to premium clients only, preserving privacy

**What clients see:**
- The artisan's full story, specialty, and craft technique
- A scrollable gallery of their finest work
- Their average rating and reviews from past masterclass participants
- Contact information (if the client has a premium subscription)

---

### 2. Client Discovery & Favourites

Clients can save the artisans they love and keep their own curated list.

**What clients can do:**
- Add any artisan to a personal **favourites list** with one tap
- Remove artisans from their favourites at any time
- Return to their list whenever they want to re-visit a favourite artisan's profile

---

### 3. Artisan Directory & Search

Souklab's powerful search engine makes it easy for clients to discover the right artisan for any occasion.

**What the search can do:**
- **Free-text search** — type anything (a craft name, technique, material, or artisan name)
- **Filter by craft category** — e.g. pottery, embroidery, jewellery, woodwork
- **Filter by location** — narrow results to a specific wilaya or region across Algeria
- **Filter by materials** — e.g. clay, leather, silver, wool
- **Filter by technique** — e.g. hand-painting, weaving, engraving
- **Filter by era/style** — traditional, contemporary, or historical periods
- **Filter by verified artisans only** — show only platform-verified craftspeople
- **Filter by teachers only** — show artisans who run masterclasses
- **Sort results** by relevance, name, registration date, or star rating

All discovery features are **available without an account** — anyone can browse.

### 5. Masterclasses (Formations)

Artisans who earn **Formateur** (teacher) accreditation can create and run paid masterclasses — hands-on learning sessions for aspiring craftspeople or enthusiasts.

#### For Artisans (Teachers)

**Creating a masterclass:**
- Set the title, description, duration, schedule, location, price, and maximum number of participants
- Upload a cover thumbnail image
- Attach protected course materials (documents, guides) that only enrolled participants can access — up to 10 files per masterclass
- Save as a draft, then submit for admin review when ready

**Masterclass lifecycle:**

| Stage | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Draft | Artisan builds and edits the masterclass |
| Submitted | Sent to Souklab admin for quality review |
| Approved | Admin approves — artisan publishes it |
| Published | Visible in the catalogue; open for enrolment |
| Rejected | Admin returns with feedback; artisan can revise and resubmit |

#### For Artisans (Participants)

- Browse the **published masterclass catalogue**
- Enrol in any published masterclass (subject to available seats)
- Cancel an enrolment (up to **24 hours** before the session starts)
- Download protected course materials after enrolment
- After attending, leave a **star rating and written review**

**Important safeguards:**
- A masterclass creator cannot enrol in their own session
- Each artisan can only enrol once per masterclass
- When all seats are filled, further enrolment is automatically blocked
- Reviews can only be submitted by participants who have actually attended — preventing fake reviews

---

### 6. Teacher Accreditation (Formateur Programme)

Not every artisan automatically becomes a teacher. Souklab maintains quality by requiring **explicit accreditation** before an artisan can create masterclasses.

**The application journey:**
1. Artisan submits an accreditation application through their account
2. Souklab admin reviews the application (portfolio, credentials, experience)
3. Admin **approves** → artisan can immediately create masterclasses
4. Admin **rejects** → artisan receives feedback and must wait a **14-day cooldown** before reapplying
5. Admin can also grant or revoke teacher status directly at any time

This ensures masterclasses are run by verified, capable craftspeople.

### 7. Social Feed

Souklab has a **community feed** where verified artisans can share their work, process, and stories.

**Artisans can:**
- Publish posts with text, tags, and up to **10 photos or images**
- Edit or delete their own posts at any time
- Build a following and engage with the community

**Everyone can:**
- Browse the feed
- **Like** posts (like count is visible and updated in real time)
- **Comment** on posts and reply to other comments

**Platform moderation:**
- Souklab staff can hide, publish, or remove any post that violates platform guidelines

---

### 8. Reviews & Ratings

Artisans build their reputation through an honest, verified review system.

- Only artisans who have **attended** a masterclass can leave a review
- Reviews include a **star rating** and a written comment
- Artisans can update or delete their own reviews
- The artisan's **average star rating** on their profile updates automatically as reviews are added or changed
- One review per attended session — no duplicates allowed

---

### 9. Content Reporting

Souklab provides a community-powered safety mechanism.

**Anyone can report:**
- A feed post
- A comment
- Another user's account

**Report reasons include:** Spam, harassment, inappropriate content, misinformation, and others.

**Admin resolution options:**
- Dismiss the report (no action needed)
- Remove the reported content
- Suspend or ban the reported account

This keeps the platform safe and trustworthy for everyone.

---

### 10. Real-Time Messaging

Clients and artisans can communicate directly through Souklab's built-in messaging system — no need to share personal contact details upfront.

**Features:**
- **1-on-1 conversations** between any client and any artisan
- Send text messages and attach files in the same conversation
- See when messages have been **read** by the other person
- See live **typing indicators** ("is typing…") so you know the other person is responding
- **Edit or delete** messages after sending
- Full **message history** is preserved and accessible at any time
- Conversations persist across devices — reconnecting resumes exactly where you left off

**Real-time delivery:** Messages arrive **instantly** via WebSocket — no page refresh needed.

### 11. Notifications

Souklab keeps users informed about everything that matters to them.

**Events that trigger notifications include:**

| Event | Who Receives It |
|---|---|
| Account approved or rejected by admin | The account holder |
| Account suspended or reinstated | The account holder |
| Masterclass approved or rejected by admin | The artisan teacher |
| New masterclass available matching interests | Artisans in the catalogue |
| New message received | The recipient |
| Subscription renewed, expired, or revoked | The subscriber |
| Subscription renewal reminder (7 days and 1 day before expiry) | The subscriber |
| Payment success or failure | The payer |
| New content report received | Souklab admin |
| New review left on a masterclass | The artisan teacher |
| Formateur accreditation request submitted/approved/rejected/revoked | The artisan |

**Notification management:**
- View all notifications in a sorted list (newest first)
- See an **unread count badge** at a glance
- Mark individual notifications as read
- Mark all notifications as read in one action
- Delete notifications you no longer need

### 12. Subscriptions & Premium Access

Souklab operates on a **tiered subscription model** for artisans, unlocking more platform capabilities as their business grows.

| Tier | Who It's For |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Artisans getting started — basic listing and presence |
| **Pro** | Growing artisans — expanded product and masterclass limits |
| **Premium** | Established artisans — maximum visibility, priority directory placement, and full feature access |

**Premium tier also benefits clients:** Premium clients can see the full contact details (phone and email) of any artisan — enabling direct off-platform contact when needed.

**How subscriptions work:**
- Artisans choose a plan, pay via Chargily (Algeria's leading payment gateway)
- Subscriptions renew automatically with advance reminders
- Artisans can cancel at any time
- Souklab admin can manually grant, revoke, or correct subscription status

### 13. Avatars & Profile Photos

Every user on Souklab can manage their profile photo (avatar).

- Upload up to **10 avatar images** per account
- Choose which avatar is currently displayed on the profile
- Delete any avatar that is no longer needed
- Switching avatars is instant — no delay

**Safety:** All uploaded images are automatically scanned for viruses and validated for file type to keep the platform secure.

### 14. Account Safety & Security

Souklab takes account security seriously with multiple layers of protection.

**Account access:**
- Email address + password login, or **sign in with Google**
- **Two-step email verification** when registering — a 6-digit code sent to your inbox
- If the code is entered wrong 5 times, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes
- **Automatic login lockout** after 5 failed password attempts — unlocks after 15 minutes

**Password security:**
- Passwords must meet minimum length and complexity requirements
- **Forgot password?** Souklab sends a secure one-time reset code by email — safe against account enumeration
- Changing your password immediately invalidates all other active sessions

**Session management:**
- Login sessions expire automatically after 24 hours of inactivity
- Logging out immediately ends that session — other devices remain logged in
- Logging in with a new device creates a fresh, independent session

### 15. Admin Dashboard & Platform Moderation

The Souklab operations team has a full set of tools to run the platform smoothly.

**User management:**
- View all user accounts, filter by type and status
- Approve pending registrations individually or in bulk
- Temporarily time-out a user (account automatically reactivates when the period ends)
- Permanently ban an account
- Unban or lift a timeout at any time
- Full **audit log** of every admin action — who did what and when

**Masterclass moderation:**
- Review submitted masterclasses before they go live
- Approve or reject with written feedback
- Publish approved masterclasses

**Content moderation:**
- Review reported posts, comments, and user accounts
- Dismiss false reports, remove content, or escalate to user suspension

**Formateur governance:**
- Review teacher accreditation applications
- Approve or reject with configurable cooldown
- Directly grant or revoke teacher status
- Lift cooldowns early when appropriate

**Subscription management:**
- View all subscriber accounts and payment history
- Manually grant or revoke subscriptions
- Process refunds
- Correct subscription or payment states when needed

**Catalog & Taxonomy governance:**
- Create, update, and manage craftsmanship techniques, historical epoques, and Algerian geographic regions
- Maintain two-tier hierarchies for trade categories & subcategories (French craft taxonomy) and Mediterranean material families & materials
- Automatic slug derivation and conflict detection ensure consistent URL and reference keys
- Deletion guards protect integrity: parent categories or material families with active children cannot be accidentally removed
- Immutable audit log captures every catalog modification with administrative actor details

---

### 16. Platform Analytics (Operations Team)

Souklab provides an internal analytics engine for tracking platform health and growth.

**What can be measured:**
- User registration and activation trends
- Masterclass enrolment and attendance rates
- Revenue and subscription metrics over time
- Content publishing and engagement rates

**How it works:**
- Data is aggregated into configurable time buckets: daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly
- Analytics jobs run in the background and results are accessible without waiting
- Results can be **exported to CSV** for use in external tools (Excel, Google Sheets, etc.)
- Up to **2 years of rollup data** is retained for trend analysis
